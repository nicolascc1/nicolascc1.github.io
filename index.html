Cheng, Chong 程冲

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at HKUST (Guangzhou & Clear Water Bay), advised by Prof. Hao Wang and Prof. Long Chen. I was a visiting student at NTU working with Prof. Guosheng Lin. I received my M.Sc. from TU Berlin.

My research focuses on 3D/4D foundation models and world models. Starting from 3D scene representation, I have progressively expanded to long-horizon streaming autoregressive modeling. Currently, I am exploring unified representations that connect spatial reasoning, dynamic modeling, and understanding and generation, toward building unified multimodal world models.

I am graduating soon and actively looking for job opportunities. Feel free to reach out!
